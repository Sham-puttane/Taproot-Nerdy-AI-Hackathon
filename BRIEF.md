# The brief, verbatim

Fetched from https://hackathon.nerdy.com/ on 9 Sept 2026. Kept in the repo
because until now the requirements existed only as my paraphrase of them,
which is how a submission quietly drifts off the thing it is answering.

## Prompt 01 — the one we are answering

> "Develop an interactive, gamified math experience tailored for elementary
> students that makes foundational arithmetic concepts both intuitive and
> engaging."

They are looking for:

> "innovative mechanics that encourage steady progression and reward mastery
> of core numeracy skills."

## Judging

There is **no published rubric and no weights.** The stated bar across all
prompts is "the same rigor" — **real learners, real problems, demoable
solutions.**

Read that literally: this is judged as a PRODUCT, by people who will watch a
short video. Nothing in the brief asks for an architecture. Anything we want
credited for has to be either visible in the demo or written down in the
description field below.

## Required for submission

- name and email
- **a description of what was built and the construction approach**  <- REQUIRED
- a 2–3 minute demo video (YouTube, Loom, Drive or Vimeo)
- the prompt category

## Optional

- code repository link
- live demo
- additional materials

## Deadline

**Friday, 18 September 2026, 11:59pm CDT.**

## Prizes

$10,000 winner, $5,000 runner-up. All finalists get visibility with Nerdy
engineering leadership — which is the part that matters if the goal is the
AI Product Engineer role rather than the cash.
